Question 2 — which site loses it

The line renders twice for the same card on one screen: once in the card band under the 44px figure, once in the copies row about 500px away. That is the repetition you named. The two agents split on which one goes, and both arguments are real.

Agent A — drop it from the band

The walked card always has a row in the copies list below-right, so the band’s copy is the redundant one. This matches your screenshot, which crops the band. It buys the copies list no height — they are separate grid columns — so it is purely a repetition fix.

Agent B — drop it from the copies row

The band renders once per screen; the copies row renders once per copy, and your store has a SKU with seven. Seven near-identical grey blocks is the dense-table failure DESIGN.md names by the front door — and the copies row answers which box, not which slot.

My read is A — you cropped the band, and the two pairs you could not scan (Galio/Evelynn, Kha’Zix/Poppy) are both from the copies rows, so the copies rows are the thing to fix and the band is the thing to drop. Agent B’s cardinality point is what the ladder plus the deleted gap clause is paying for.

One catch before I build

Inventory.tsx’s lone-copy branch — a card with no name and no SKU — draws no copies row at all. Its own comment puts that at 22% of the store, and 629 of 682 records when it was written. For those cards the band is currently the only place the neighbours appear. So if the band loses the line, that branch has to gain it, or D30 breaks for most of your boxes. I’ll do that as part of the same change rather than leave it as a follow-up.
