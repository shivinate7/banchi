Spiritforged card sort

All 221 Spiritforged cards, sorted by how competitive play treats them. The aim: tell which abundant cards can never rise, which are played whatever the meta, and which move with it. This is a reading of tournament play to inform your own decisions, not price advice.

The answer in five groups

How to read each group

staple In 8% or more of top decks in every window since March, and no single legend holds more than half its decks. Demand does not depend on one deck. The 2028 rotation of Sets 1 to 5 is its ceiling, and the dates of that rotation are disputed.

meta-dependent Play rises and falls with which legends win, or is tied to one legend. Each row names what would have to change for it to rise. Rising play with a flat price is the case to watch. Falling play is a reason to sell while the price holds, unless you expect the named change.

outclassed Another legal card does the same job better and gets played. The row names it. Value is unlikely to rise unless that card is banned. Bulk or sell.

banned Banned in Standard since 31 Mar 2026. Only an unban would bring it back.

unknown The evidence is too thin to call. Most were never in a top deck, but no better card could be named either. Nothing on this page is a guess.

How this was done

Play data. 402 unique top decks from 11 official Regional Qualifiers. Each one came from Riot's own Top Decks articles at playriftbound.com, read as raw decklists. Each article gives the best deck for each legend plus the top 8, so the sample holds about one deck per legend per event. A share here means how widely a card is used across legends. It is not the card's share of the whole field.

Sorting. A first pass grouped cards by play counts alone. Then four judges, each covering one group of domains, read every card's rules text, cost and play counts. Each judge named the card that beats it, or what would have to change for it to rise. A fifth agent was asked to refute the judges' calls, and it overturned 11 of them. Most of those 11 had named a "better" card that does a different job, so they became unknown. Last, every staple was held to one stated test, and 8 failed it. Rengar, Irelia Fervent, Ruin Runner and B.F. Sword, among others, moved to meta-dependent.

Ban history. Three waves, on 31 Mar, 24 Jul and 18 Sep 2026. Two Spiritforged cards are banned: Called Shot and Draven, Vanquisher. Source: riftbound.zone's ban list (4 Oct 2026), checked against Riot's ban articles.

Known gaps

72 unknowns, plus Temptation. Most were never in a top deck, but no played card clearly does their job better. The sorting skipped Temptation (SFD-129), so its unknown is a default, not a judgment.
The latest window is one event. W4 is Los Angeles alone, 36 decks, so one deck moves a share by about 3 points. Any "rising" call that rests on W4 alone is weak. Trusty Ramhound and Sterak's Gage are two of them.
The sample cannot show a legend growing. It holds about one deck per legend per event, so a legend's share stays flat by construction. Many "what must change" lines read "legend X gains share", and this data cannot confirm that happening. Riftdecks' metashare pages could, but they refused every request.
Radiance is not covered. It came out on 23 Oct 2026 and has had no qualifier yet. It may push out cards sorted here.
China is not covered. Chinese events have no official decklists, so none are counted.
Reprints blur the counts. About 16 card names are also printed in other sets (Origins, Unleashed, Vendetta). Play counts cannot tell the printings apart, and a reprint shares supply, which caps how far the Spiritforged print can rise. These rows are flagged.
One price source. Every price comes from tcgcsv.com's mirror of TCGplayer, with no second source to check against.
Rotation is disputed. Riot's rules page says rotation starts in 2028. Sheep Esports reports Sets 1 to 5 leaving then. The sources disagree on how it works.
Fizz, Trickster stays a staple on judgment. It is a champion card, but its decks span many legends, with no legend above 19%. Whether any legend may play it is unverified.
