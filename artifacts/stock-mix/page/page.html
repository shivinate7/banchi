How each measure is counted
  • Cards captured: every card photographed, whether it is on hand, sold or not listed yet. Cards you retired or moved are left out.
  • Sold of captured: sold cards divided by captured cards, over all time.
  • Weeks of stock: cards on hand divided by the weekly pace of the last 14 days. A dash means nothing sold in that time.
  • Not listed yet cards count by the rarity you claimed at capture.
  • Revenue: gross from completed orders, never profit. Median price: the sale price for sold cards, the market price for the rest.
