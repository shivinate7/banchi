Where price history shows

Each option draws the same pricing list with Gardevoir ex's history open. The chart and numbers are samples. Pick the one you want to use while you price.

Width Theme

1. Keep the sheet

Today's history sheet slides in from the right and dims the list.
Gain
Nothing new to build. It is today's sheet plus two marks: your asking price and the suggestion.
Loss
The list is covered. You cannot see the row's price, its tick or the next row while the history is open.
Open full size

2. The row opens in place

A panel opens under the row and pushes the rows below down.
Gain
The history sits right beside the price it explains, and the rest of the list stays in view.
Loss
The rows below move down when you press it. The list gets longer. The daily copies-sold bars are not drawn.
Open full size

3. A side panel follows your row

At 1440 a panel beside the list shows whichever row is selected.
Gain
The history changes as you move down the list with the arrow keys. No press per row.
Loss
The list loses its trend column to make room. At 820 there is no room, so it falls back to the sheet. No daily copies-sold bars at 1440.
Open full size

4. A card floats over the list

Your pickIt opens by itself when a price box takes focus, above that row, so the next price stays clear. Nothing dims, and nothing moves.
Gain
The list never moves and stays visible. You can press another row and the card follows it. Its chart is wider than the sheet's, and it works the same at both widths.
Loss
While open, it covers the right side of the few rows under it: their prices and buttons, never their names. No daily copies-sold bars. The sold range stays on the row.
