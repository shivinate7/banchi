Each slab's Card Ladder value against the same card raw in Near Mint on TCGplayer.
Card Ladder value: their estimate for the slab at its grade. "No comps" means Card Ladder had no sale and showed your $100 placeholder. Conf: Card Ladder's confidence in its own value, 1 to 5. At 2 or less the value is struck through and the slab is priced by the median of its last sales at the grade. Last sale: the newest sale at this grade; hover for the last five. Raw NM: TCGplayer market price for the ungraded card. Hover a price for the product it came from, or "none" for why there is none. PSA 10: PriceCharting's PSA 10 price for the same card, matched by its TCGplayer product. Hover for the page. Graded: every slab of this card at this grader. Rate: the share at this slab's grade. Top %: the share at this grade or higher, so a small number is a scarce slab. Best %: the share at the highest grade this card has reached at this grader. Hover for the whole ladder. Ask: the higher of the two, so a slab worth less than its raw card is priced at the card.
