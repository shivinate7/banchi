- orders-plumbing-findings: A read-only bug hunt of the Orders screen found nine plumbing bugs. (1) The walk plan drops owed copies when one SKU spans sections (`pipeline/walkplan.py`). (2) A sale is refused after a return read, because `useOrderWalk`'s `tallyRef` subtracts sales the plan already nets. (3) `undoPullNow` skips the refresh, so positions go off by one. (4) `/orders/pull` accepts a sale on a canceled order or a stood-down line. (5) A 409 `already_sold` leaves a stale Mark Sold and a raw JSON toast. (6) Focus drops to body when `RowAction` swaps buttons. (7) `U` does nothing after clicking another row; `U` is to undo the newest standing sale (undo spec §10). (8) Row Undo stays disabled across the post-sale re-reads. (9) `U` during an in-flight sale shows nothing (low confidence). Fix lanes: `lane/walkplan-owed-stops`, `lane/orders-pull-status`, `lane/orders-undo-plumbing`, `lane/walk-pane-tally-focus`.
