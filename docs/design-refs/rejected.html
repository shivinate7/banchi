What was rejected, and what it looked like

docs/DESIGN.md names several things this design is not. A negative constraint is only useful if a later session can see what it is refusing — otherwise it reads as taste and gets overturned by the first person with different taste. Every panel here was rendered, looked at, and turned down on 2026-08-12. The chosen design is in locked.html. Cards are synthetic rectangles at geometry/detect.py's CARD_ASPECT; the pre-commit hook blocks images outside captures/.

Grounds

The first pass was built on warm paper with a high-contrast serif display. It reads NYT / FT — sanitised, editorial, professional in a way that belongs to a newspaper and not to a workbench. That verdict is the reason DESIGN.md bans newspaper coding by name rather than merely specifying a hex value.

It was still rejected, and DESIGN.md records that no rule about header strips should be written either way — this is a rejected element, not a banned category.

Where the solid fill goes

Accent works at two weights: outline for unsure, solid fill for the only action. On a two-answer screen the fill needs a rule, and both available rules are worse than having none. Filling the pricier row biases the queue toward over-listing. Filling the toggle's answer is defensible under D3 but makes one fill mean two things.
