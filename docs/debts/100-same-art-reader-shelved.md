## DEBT100 — the free number reader for same-art printings is shelved

**What.** Two printings of one card can share their art. The free image reader cannot tell them apart. A free reader of the printed collector number would. It is not built, and the owner shelved it. Same-art printings stay a human check in Review.

- **Result:** each card with a same-art sibling needs a person to choose the printing. No free step settles it.
- **Not fixed because** the owner shelved the reader.
- **Closes when** the owner lifts the shelf, a free reader of the printed number is built for these printings, and it is measured against the owner's own answers in Review.
- **Points to** `docs/specs/identify-engine-pick.md`, DEBT64 (the collector number's printed total is not cross-checked against the local catalog) and DEBT65 (the number-corner crop is sent only on a retry).

**Outcome at risk.** The owner keeps making one human check per same-art card, on every box that holds one.
