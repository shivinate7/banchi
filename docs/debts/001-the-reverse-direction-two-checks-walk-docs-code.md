## 1 — The reverse direction: two checks walk docs→code and never back

**What.** The map check proves that what is published is consistent. It cannot see a real thing that was never published.

- **A decisions line.** The map may not know less than the code's own citations, and may say more. A line can list a decision its file does not implement. Telling which citations count needs a guess, and D16 keeps a guess off a blocking row.

**Outcome at risk.** A green row reads as coverage. A decision listed without being implemented passes.

**Closes when.** A decision rules that "a test imports it" does or does not mean "a test covers it", and settles whether `governed_by` may be checked from the map side.
