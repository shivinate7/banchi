## 25 — A cited number still resolves when it is the wrong one, and no check can read what a sentence is about

`make docs-audit` proves every `DEBT<n>` and `D<n>` in the tree names a real entry (`debt ids`, `decision ids`) and that every debts heading parses (`debts headings`). It cannot prove the entry cited is the one the sentence means, because a wrong id resolves as well as a right one. Seven citations once named the capture-server entry for a record that lived in another. A row would have to decide what a sentence is about. The nearest proxy compared a quoted phrase against the entry cited. Over the 38 citations then in the tree, it gave one catch and three false alarms. The most-repeated distinctive phrase was spelled differently in the correct entry. A hand-kept (phrase, entry) list is a claim with an unread reader.

**Outcome at risk.** A reader follows a citation to a real entry that does not say what the sentence claims.

**Closes when.** Nothing mechanical is known. Cite by narrating, and re-read the citations of an entry whose subject changes.
