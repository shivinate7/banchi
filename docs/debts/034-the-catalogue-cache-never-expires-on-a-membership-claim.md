## 34 — Categories and products never expire, on a membership claim this store cannot re-check on its own

`pipeline/pricehistory.py:CATALOG_TTL_SECONDS` is `float("inf")` for categories and product lists. The groups list expires daily (`GROUPS_TTL_SECONDS`). A group or product miss refetches once per `Market` before refusing. So a new set or card reaches the store without a manual delete. A refetch that returns fewer results than the cache held is not stored. The owner ruled that the cards in a catalogue set do not change between pulls. Under that ruling, recently-modified products keep their original low product ids, and no new ids appear. Churn is a field edit to an existing record. `Market.prices` keeps its own `PRICE_TTL_SECONDS`, because a daily price must not become permanent. Unmeasured: whether the mirror ever corrects a collector number on an existing card, which is the one field `pipeline/join.py` reads off the catalogue. A diff of `by_number` keys between two snapshots of one group, a week apart, settles it at no request cost.

**Outcome at risk.** A wrong or partial cached category or product list is never repaired by expiry, only by a miss. Fixing one means deleting a derived directory.

**Closes when.** Cache size on disk becomes material. The store covers enough sets that one bad entry is no longer cheap to notice by hand. Or the diff above finds a corrected number.
