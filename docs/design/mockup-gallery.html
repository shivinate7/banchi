Mockup gallery

Every explore mockup on branch design-mock/gallery. All of it is proposals: nothing is built or ruled. Blind = drew from the job alone; informed = saw today's screen; directed = informed plus one named direction.

Feature ideas from the planner run

Cards to Pull (web)

  • pull-blind-1 blind
    Three concepts: Shelf walk (one card at a time in shelf order, into numbered envelopes), Shelf map (the six boxes drawn, dots per card), Envelope stack.
    notes
  • pull-informed-1 informed
    One row per card, not per copy: each says "Pull N" and names the envelope; at 1440 the next card opens beside the list, so Pull is on the first screen.
    notes
  • pull-directed-1 directed buyer-first: one envelope at a time
    The envelope is the unit of work: one buyer's cards in box order, "Pick N", Undo beside the press, "Next envelope" never opens by itself.
    notes
  • pull-directed-2 directed the route first: the walk along the boxes as a map
    The walk plan's stops become the list: each box a line, each stop a numbered station; at a stop "Pull 2", Pull, Mark It Sold, then the buyer's envelope. Unboxed cards sit in their envelope as "Keep open".
    notes

iPhone app: pull, find, slabs

  • phone-pull-blind-1 blind
    Three iPhone concepts: Walk (one route in shelf order), Envelopes (one buyer at a time), Shelf map (the shelf from above; search lights up copies).
    notes
  • phone-pull-informed-1 informed
    Walk by box, but each card says "Pull 2" with the initials of the buyers it goes to, so cards drop straight into envelopes at the shelf. Pull and the orders walk merge into one tab.
    notes
  • phone-pull-directed-1 directed camera check: the phone confirms the card in hand
    The camera reads name and number; Mark It Sold appears only after both match, so a wrong card cannot be sold. Compare by Eye is the fallback.
    notes
  • phone-pull-directed-2 directed one thumb while walking
    One card per screen with two fixed thumb slots (Pull above, Mark Sold below); "Take 3" and numbered envelopes do the sorting.
    notes

Pricing

  • pricing-blind-1 blind
    Three concepts: Desk (one card at a time, send totals on the right), Band (drag a range on a -60% to +60% line; changes inside it send without a look), Manifest (the next send written out as a document).
    notes
  • pricing-informed-1 informed
    The send bar is one fixed 80px line in every state, so it never jumps (DEBT89-91); every count is in copies; row 1 starts at 308px instead of 753px.
    notes
  • pricing-directed-1 directed one question at a time
    Pricing opens on one question ("Market fell 38% since you listed it. Lower it to $31.44?"), with priced answer buttons, a queue beside it, a fixed one-line send bar and copies as the one unit; the table is the second view.
    notes
