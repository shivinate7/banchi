Filling today's orders on the phone

Designer kind: blind. Designer: phone-pull-blind-1. Three ways to answer one job: take the owed cards off the shelf, check each against its photo, put it in its buyer's envelope, mark it sold, and name what could not be found. Every button works. Each concept is drawn folded (one hand, at the shelf) and unfolded (on the packing table); a tap in either pose moves both.
