The contract

  1. Tile size, columns, scroll extent and the newest tile's place stay as the figures say, at 1440 and 820.
  2. A capture adds one tile at rank 0. Every older tile steps one cell, as today, with no animation. Only the new tile fades in. Tiles at rank 15 and past never animate.
  3. The rail's rows never drop during a sitting. Ghost cells hold the height after a single-card remove.
  4. The head line, the empty dispenser line and the undo note keep their reserved lines. In hand-fed mode the dispenser line is empty.
  5. Every tile of the sitting is a real row: Tab reaches it, a screen reader counts it, and a scroll shows its photo with no blank frame.
  6. A tile that scrolls in or takes focus shows its live number and Undo label before it can be read or pressed. Its press undoes exactly that many cards.
  7. Unmeasured today: where the view sits when a capture lands while the rail is scrolled down. The fix keeps today's behaviour. The design reviewer measures it before and after.

Design reviewer, after the build

  • At 5, 20 and 60 captures, at 1440 and 820, in both themes: the figures match this page.
  • Across one capture, the rail's top and every panel below it hold still.
  • A fast scroll to the oldest tile shows no blank tile and no stale number.
  • Tab reaches the oldest tile. Its label and number are live.
  • The spec that counts 36 rows and the spec that presses rank 20 pass unedited, or are re-pointed with a reason.
  • The stability spec passes for Capture at 1440 and 820.
