# Feature ideas: proposals for review

New feature directions for Banchi, not redesigns of existing screens. Nothing here is ruled on or
built. Each is a proposal for the owner to read, click through, keep or drop.

How they were made: blind Opus planners each got a plain-words picture of the business and one lens
(`briefs.md`), and no screens, code or specs. Each returned three directions. The strongest went back
to their planner for a deeper pass ("went further"), and some then went to an Opus designer for a
clickable mockup, drawn in the `--bn-*` tokens. Card, set, buyer and seller names are made up.

Status: **mockup** (clickable HTML and notes), **further** (deeper plan), **plan** (round-1 sketch).

## Themes several blind planners reached on their own

- **Sorted order is free evidence** (rig, automate, learning, photos): check identity with it and pay less for identify.
- **Positions drift after pulls** (space, ambient, learning, tenx, pride): neighbours, tombstones, census, or fixed addresses.
- **Photo at packing, for disputes** (wild, product, condition, fulfiller, supplies, pride).
- **One value line splits bulk from the few that matter** (slabs, market, subtract, iphone).
- **The owner away** (resilience, handover, attention): set calls ahead with cut-offs.
