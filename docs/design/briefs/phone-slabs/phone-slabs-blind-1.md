Designer: phone-slabs-blind-1

## Job
The owner keeps a ledger of graded cards sealed in plastic cases, each with a label: grading company, grade and a certificate number. They add one by holding the case up to the phone, and they want to see what the whole ledger and each case are worth today. Done looks like: the case added with no typing, its value shown, and the ledger total current.

Things the job handles:
- Up to about 400 graded cases; about 4 grading companies; grades 1 to 10 with halves.
- Each case: card name, set, number, grade, certificate number, a photo, what was paid and today's value.
- The records live on a Mac at home, reached over the home network; away from home it may be out of reach.
- The owner will click through every concept: each press, sheet, state and step works in the page. Use made-up names and cards only. Drawings only: build nothing, open no pull request, commit and push after each pass to the branch `design-mock/phone-slabs-blind-1`.

## Device
iPhone Duo, folded: size not written, draw at a typical modern iPhone size and say so. Then iPhone Duo, unfolded: size not written, draw at the unfolded size you choose and say so. Touch only. Follow skill `ios-apps`.

## Deliver
Start from the job, not from a product you know. You see no current screen, on purpose. Draw at least two concepts that answer the job at the root, each with its own structure, its own way to move around and its own first screen. Make one concept deliberately bold: a structure that no common app of this kind uses. Write `<designer>.html` and `<designer>-notes.md` in the design folder, where `<designer>` is the value of the Designer line. Read no other file in the design folder.
