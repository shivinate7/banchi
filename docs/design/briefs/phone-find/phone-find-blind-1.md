## Job
The owner stands at the shelf with a phone and asks "where is this card, and how many do I have?" Then they sell, retire or move one copy right there. Sometimes they hold the card and want the phone to recognise it. Done looks like: the card found, its spot shown, the change made with a way back.

Things the job handles:
- About 3,600 cards in about 6 boxes of 545 to 732 cards, 9 to 22 labelled sections each, with a position number.
- One product may have several copies in different boxes, each with its own photo and condition.
- The records live on a Mac at home, reached over the home network; away from home it may be out of reach.
- The owner will click through every concept: each press, sheet, state and step works in the page. Use made-up names and cards only. Drawings only: build nothing, open no pull request, commit and push after each pass to the branch `design-mock/phone-find-blind-1`.

## Device
iPhone Duo, folded: size not written, draw at a typical modern iPhone size and say so. Then iPhone Duo, unfolded: size not written, draw at the unfolded size you choose and say so. Touch only. Follow skill `ios-apps`.

## House rules
Read `~/.claude/docs/design/principles.md`, `~/.claude/docs/design/layout.md` and `~/.claude/docs/design/tokens.md`. Take type, spacing and colour from `tokens.md`, and where it gives no value, choose your own. Use no colour, font or spacing of any product or repo. A repo name in a house file is a credit, not a place to look.

## Deliver
Start from the job, not from a product you know. You see no current screen, on purpose. Draw at least two concepts that answer the job at the root, each with its own structure, its own way to move around and its own first screen. Make one concept deliberately bold: a structure that no common app of this kind uses. Write `<designer>.html` and `<designer>-notes.md` in the design folder, where `<designer>` is the value of the Designer line. Read no other file in the design folder.
