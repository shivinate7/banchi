Designer: capture-blind-1

## Job
The owner sits at a rig: a camera looks down on a tray, and a card dispenser drops one card at a time onto it. Before a run they pick which storage box and which labelled section the cards are going into. Then every card is photographed as it lands, at about 1,300 an hour, while the owner's hands load the dispenser and watch for jams. Done looks like: every card photographed once, in the right box and section and order, with bad shots caught and redone at once, and the sitting closed.

Things the job handles:
- A box takes 545 to 732 cards in 9 to 22 sections; a section break is marked as cards flow.
- 802 cards in 37 minutes on a good sitting; a jam, a double drop or a blurred shot must be undone without losing the place.
- A quick free reader names most cards as they land; the owner only needs to know if something is wrong.
- The screen is a few steps away from the owner's eyes; hands are busy.
- The owner will click through every concept: each press, sheet, state and step works in the page. Use made-up names and cards only. Drawings only: build nothing, open no pull request, commit and push after each pass to the branch `design-mock/capture-blind-1`.

## Device
desktop width: 1440. Then 820 (a tablet or narrow window). Light and dark themes. Pointer at 1440; touch at 820.

## Deliver
Start from the job, not from a product you know. You see no current screen, on purpose. Draw at least two concepts that answer the job at the root, each with its own structure, its own way to move around and its own first screen. Make one concept deliberately bold: a structure that no common app of this kind uses. Write `<designer>.html` and `<designer>-notes.md` in the design folder, where `<designer>` is the value of the Designer line. Read no other file in the design folder.
