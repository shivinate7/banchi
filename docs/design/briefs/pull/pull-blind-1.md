## Job
A helper who is not technical fills the day's card orders. The owner's rule for this person: "if a flow needs explaining twice, redesign it." The helper stands at a shelf of long storage boxes with a screen beside them. They must find out which cards to take, how many of each, from which box and spot, and which buyer each one goes to. Done looks like: every owed card is in its buyer's envelope, each one checked against a photo of the card and marked sold, and the cards that could not be found are named for the owner.

Things the job handles:
- Orders: a median of 3 a day, 11 on a busy day, 39 at the peak. Each order holds a median of 1 card, up to 43.
- Boxes: about 6, each with 545 to 732 cards in 9 to 22 labelled sections. Each card has a position number within its section.
- A card in an order may want 2 of 3 copies on hand, and the copies may sit in different boxes. The helper takes only as many as are owed.
- Each stored copy has a photo of the exact card, taken when it was filed.
- Some owed cards have no known spot; the owner must hear about them.
- The owner will click through every concept: each press, sheet, state and step works in the page, so the owner can try it before choosing. Use made-up names and cards only. Drawings only: build nothing, open no pull request, and push your work to the branch `design-mock/pull-blind-1`.

## Device
desktop width: 1440. Then 820 (a tablet or narrow window). Light and dark themes. Pointer at 1440; touch is likely at 820, with the screen propped beside the boxes and hands often full.

## Deliver
Start from the job, not from a product you know. You see no current screen, on purpose. Draw at least two concepts that answer the job at the root, each with its own structure, its own way to move around and its own first screen. Make one concept deliberately bold: a structure that no common app of this kind uses. Write `<designer>.html` and `<designer>-notes.md` in the design folder, where `<designer>` is the value of the Designer line. Read no other file in the design folder.
