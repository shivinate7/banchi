Phone Pull, One Thumb

Designer kind: directed. Direction: one thumb while walking: one glanceable next card at a time, every control in the thumb zone.

The idea: the walk is one card on one screen. Read top to bottom: where it is, how many to take, the photo, then two thumb slots that never move. The upper slot pulls, the lower slot sells, so a double tap can never sell a card whose photo was not seen. Press anything in the frames: each tap, sheet and step works.
