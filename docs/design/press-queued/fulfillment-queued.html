Does the kit Button change today's look?

Yes, at rest and on hover. Measured in a scratch page that loads the real kit.css and then Fulfillment.css, at 1100 wide in light. With bn-btn added and nothing else:

  • Receipt Undo. A 1px border makes it 2px wider (124.4 to 126.4). The kit's box shadow draws a ring at rest. On hover the ink fill turns to --bn-btn-bg-hover and the label, in --bn-bg, disappears: .bn-btn:hover outranks .ff-undo, which sets no hover ground.
  • Row Undo. The label drops from 20px to 13px (104.4 to 90.3 wide), under the view's 20px floor. It gains the ring at rest, the border, and the kit's 0.99 press squeeze.
  • Both. The kit's icon prop draws the glyph at 16px. Today's glyphs are 22px and 18px, so the glyph stays a child Icon.

Kept: the normal state below is today's look, with the overrides in the mockup's "NEW: what Fulfillment.css adds" block. The press squeeze is the kit's press floor (D50) and stays.
