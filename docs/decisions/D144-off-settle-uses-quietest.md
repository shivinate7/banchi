## D144 — Off-settle cards use the quietest frame

**A card that will not settle is photographed off the quietest frame it manages, and there is one trigger only.** A stall is a card. Under a feeder a soft photograph beats none. The card is about to be replaced. `U` undoes a bad one in one press. A lost card surfaces weeks later in a box that does not match its run.

- **The rescue.** Once a settle episode has run `rescueAfter` times `maxMoveMs` without settling, a frame under `rescueK` times `tLo` settles it. The window requirement is dropped. The verdict is `fire:rescued`. It is a distinct string, because the photograph differs in kind and the rig run must be able to count them. `rescue` joins `stall` on the HUD. `rescueK` is 4/3, `sqrt(moveK / stillK)`, the geometric center of the Schmitt band and the largest value the corpus tolerates. `rescueAfter` is 0.60, where two plateaux meet in a sweep of 0.50 to 0.80. Both live in `app/src/motion.ts`, mirrored in `scripts/score-trace.py` and locked by `make docs-audit`'s `motion params` row.
- **It is not a threshold problem.** Every stall episode's quietest frame sat between 1.02 and 1.25 times `tLo`. Nine of the first fourteen were under it, refused by `stillWindow` alone. (The card landed a frame or two after its transit, and the next card's motion threw the window away.) Dropping the window requirement rescues them. It also takes double counts from 2 to 21. `stillWindow` stops a dip during a card's transit from firing. A second fire on one card is worse than a miss. A ramp from `tLo` over the stall clock is the same defect. A stillness rule that buys cards on one rig by spending them on another is this subsystem's history (D81's brightness floor).
- **Presence, novelty and the refractory are not relaxed.** A rescue cannot photograph the bare stand (D84's two junk rows stay fixed) or the same card twice. Over the three banked evening sessions the corpus goes from 122 photographs and 11 stalls to 136 and 6. The six survivors are the finding: episodes whose quietest frame never came near the bar, and the machine is right to refuse them.
- **A stall is a sentence on the stage,** with a `Set aside` press that spends the count, not a number in a collapsed disclosure. It is the one event that costs a card.
- **There is no `stalled:flare` verdict.** Three discriminators all failed over the corpus. Saturation reads about 0, because a 38x28 cell averages roughly 3,600 sensor pixels and hides a clipped streak. Spike shape goes the wrong way. "Brighter than any fired card" flags 14% to 55% of ordinary episodes. `scripts/score-trace.py stalls` prints the reading and names nothing. The fix for glare is optical.
- **The cadence trigger is deleted.** A settle trigger that encodes the feeder's beat is the predictive clock-locked design D19 rejected, so nothing came across. D131's rulings 1 and 2 stand. D141's path list is derived from what the suite loads. So the `harness/traces/` entry went when no spec read a trace. The browser-scope selftest flips with it (a trace-only change now skips). A trace-only change cannot reach a browser, and any change under `app/` still triggers a run.
- **Not established.** No photograph taken under the rescue has been looked at. A trace stores pixels only for verdict frames and keyframes, so the newly fired frames are mostly not in the files. Five that were rendered at 38x28 show a card-shaped object with structure, which is a sanity check and not a focus measurement. The first armed run's checklist is in `docs/specs/motion-trigger.md` section 7.
