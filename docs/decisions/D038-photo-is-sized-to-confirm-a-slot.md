## D38 — Photo is sized to confirm a slot

**The card photograph on `#/inventory` is sized to confirm a slot (where is this card), not to judge a card.** Judging is the review queue's job at full size, and borrowing a floor from a screen with a different job is the mistake `docs/DESIGN.md` names.

- **The `<img>` carries the `aspect-ratio`, never a wrapper.** A reservation moved onto a wrapper is given to everything the wrapper can hold. A missing file drew a card-shaped box around a paragraph.
- **`object-fit: cover` stays.** Frames are 2160x3840 with the card filling 80-88% of the width and 61-72% of the height, so `contain` would letterbox and shrink the card.
- **The walk is the sections list.** The box's identity and operations live in the walk's column, and the sections are not rendered twice. `.browse-map` scrolls itself, so an editor opened past the viewport cap never puts its Apply button off screen, and `.browse-list` keeps a 6rem floor. `BoxOps` caps section rows at six and scrolls, with the total on the heading. A grid row is as tall as its tallest cell, so a tall neighbor leaves whitespace before the copies.
- **The copies list spans the content column and keys to a container query,** not a breakpoint. If the last two rows must swap, never restore a column.
- **The panel shows what a control can overwrite.** `Rarity` and `Note` (`ClaimEditor` writes five claims, so `Correct claims` is otherwise a blind overwrite). `Run` and `Confidence`, shown flat and never as a chip, because T1's misses are all confident. Whether the card has an open question from `GET /queues`, where the candidate count separates "answer it" from "re-shoot it". The reason labels live in `app/src/reasons.ts` and both screens import them.
- **The market price row is a join, not a record.** Every figure comes from the export (D8). The edge is card, then `run`, then that run's `pricing.json` (`GET /pipeline/runs/<name>/pricing`). It is keyed by position, never `card.sku`, which is `null` for every card not emitted. It reads once per run and is cleared with a reload. The age is never optional and reads `read 3d`, compact and fixed-width, because a bare price claims a currency the file cannot support. The row is never conditional: five outcomes, five sentences (`not joined yet`, `join this run`, `no row in this run`, `no_market_data` verbatim, and the price). Nothing writable lives here, since `#/pricing` is where a price is decided (D86).

Reopen if the owner opens the review queue to look at a card they were already looking at here. Reopen the price read if a box spans many runs.
