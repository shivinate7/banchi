## D252 — A wrong answer gets a correcting route

**A wrong answer that already went out cannot be undone (`undo_too_late`, D28), so a listed card gets its own correcting route, `POST /inventory/<box>/<index>/correct`.** It rebinds the SKU through `bind_sku` and never writes stored fields by hand. The SKU it leaves is over-listed by exactly what it gave up, and a departed card refuses (`card_departed`). It is not a branch on `do_review_answer`. `_answer_target` refuses `not_in_queue`, and a wrongly answered card holds no queue entry. `sku_unchanged` stays, and a sibling confirm press covers "the SKU is right and the drawn name is wrong" (D258). Undo derives the identity again and does not restore stored values.

- **The new SKU is re-read on the server** from `_catalog_for_card`'s export inside the write lock, as a D77 `from_catalog` answer is. The wire is never trusted (`sku_not_in_catalog`). It gets no listing write: choosing a row is not pushing one, and `emit` does that (D54).
- **The old SKU is released through `Listing.release` (D34).** `pushed`, `staged` and `live` are counts on a SKU and never addresses (D7). So one copy of the least-committed stage is given up: `pushed` then `staged` then `live`. `pipeline/livecheck.py:compare` (`reconcile --live`) reads the leftover into D109's `beyond` bucket, TCGplayer's own quantity for a SKU this pipeline never sent. `check_correct_answer` proves it against the real module. A live export naming the old SKU's still-live copy lands in `report.beyond`, neither `agreed` nor `unexplained`. Nothing uploads to TCGplayer, and the next `reconcile --live` says what to lower by hand.
- **The stored identity follows the catalog:** `card.name`, `set_name`, `rarity`, `number` and `printed_total`, from the same row and transaction. `_catalog_row` must carry `rarity`. It lacked the key, so `chosen.get("rarity")` always answered `None`, in this route and in `do_review_answer`'s `from_catalog` branch. T7 asserts both. Splitting the number is game-aware through the game's own key strategy: `pipeline/join.py:catalog_number_fields(game, raw_number)` reads `games.get(game)["join_key"]`. A `number_and_printed_total` game (Pokemon) splits through `store/numbers.py:split_catalog_number` on the last `/`, since a secret rare's numerator can be `302*`. Every other strategy (`printed_code`, `name_only`, `not_joined`) stores the cell verbatim with `printed_total` empty. Riftbound reads `card.number` whole. Splitting `056/298` would break a store-backed join (D188). It would also fill `number_key` wrongly and mishandle 13 double-sided `T01 // T02` cells. `_catalog_answer` returns the game beside the row, so nothing re-parses the export. `number_key` and `number_display` are never set directly (`store/master.py:_card_columns` derives them). D36 and D183 do not govern this field, and the model's own reading stays in `identifications.json`.
- **Undo goes both ways (D28's shape).** `{"undo": true}` reads back the overwritten pair. `restores_to` carries `sku`, `condition`, `set_name`, `rarity`, `name`, `number` and `printed_total`. A missing key is not a `null` claim. `_reverse_correction` checks presence, or an older line would erase a real number it never touched. The released copy goes back through the SKU's public writers. `bump` handles `pushed` and `staged`. `set` handles `live`, which `bump` refuses (D115). `staged_at` and `live_as_of` are restored at their pre-correction values. The round trip is then not a fresh reading nobody took (`check_correct_answer_live_release`). The card is the ground truth for whether there is something to reverse (its `sku` and `condition` still match the newest `sku_corrected` line). `undo_too_late` is checked on the card's current SKU. The events are `sku_corrected` and `sku_correction_undone`, never `answered` or `corrected` (D3's claim edit).
- **The screen shows on `#/inventory` only.** `app/src/CardHero.tsx`'s `ListingCorrection` sits inside `CardDetailsSection`'s `correctable` prop. That prop defaults false. It is an allow-list of one screen, and `BoxBrowse.tsx` sets it. It shows only for an identified, on-hand card. The route's own refusals are `not_identified` and `card_departed`. It reuses D77's picker (`CatalogPanel`, `CandidateButton` exported from `ReviewQueue.tsx`). A receipt with Undo follows it. Its words are "Wrong card?" and "Correct the listing." (D196). The slot keeps its height when the card leaves eligibility (D118). The wrapper always renders, and `min-height` reserves the height. A vanished control shrank the Details panel by 44px.

Correcting a sold card's SKU is a larger question (`sold_here`, a buyer's order) and is not attempted.
