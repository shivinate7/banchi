## D150 — A post-sale zero reading ages the claim

**A reading taken after a sale corroborates it.** `pushed` has no drawdown (`cmd_emit` writes it, `cmd_reconcile` moves it to `staged`, and `cmd_join` draws `staged` down by the rise in live), so once an import lands the claim counts copies TCGplayer already sold, and the copies on hand are committed against a shelf that no longer exists. A sale is the one event that proves a sent copy left. `cli/resolve.py:_copies_out` ages the claim by the SKU's sales where the export vouches for the copies having been there:

    out[sku] = max(live, claim - sold)

- **The reading answers two questions.** A reading that reports copies live vouches for every sale of the SKU, whenever it happened. A reading of nothing vouches for exactly the sales it was taken after (`Inventory.sales_before`). A zero reading four days after the copies sold is the sale's own result (D115's rule pointed the other way). A sale after a zero reading is not aged, since the reading cannot show that copy was one the claim counts. The old gate conflated "never was live" with "was live, sold, and now reads zero."
- **Neither arm double-counts.** The two are a `max` over independent estimates, never a sum, and `claim` is never decremented by a sale. Landed live with sales after it (4 @ T, 2 sold later): 2. Landed live, reading after the sales (2 @ T+): 2. Sold out, reading after the sale (0 @ T+, 1 sold): 0. Pushed, never read, 1 sold: 1. A date rule that ages only by sales before the reading answers 4 for the first and strands two copies, so this is a second arm on the gate and not a replacement.
- **Three rules, one place each.** `Listing.live_reading` picks which reading to believe, `Listing.sales_pending` says which counted sales survive it, and `Listing.reading_taken_at` dates it. Its branches mirror `live_reading` exactly, stampless included, so `_copies_out` never asks "was this sale before the reading" of a reading that was not the one used.
- **D54's re-emit idempotence stays.** Four pushed, one sold, export silent: no reading exists (`live_as_of is None`), `sales_before` counts nothing, and no fifth row is offered. Dropping the gate outright turns that case red (expected 4, actual 5). Three mutation arms were run: the old gate back, no gate, and the date comparison reversed.
- **Nothing writes, and reconcile is not the answer.** The operator does not run `reconcile --live`. The ageing is derived per resolve from two stamps the store already holds, and it also removes the false "every copy in this run is already listed" sentence, since the rows are offered.
- **The residue.** A SKU whose import landed in Staged and never went live also reads zero, and a copy sold by hand against that state ages a claim it should not. Telling it from a sold-out listing needs the marker on `Card` (D59, D147), and DEBT24 is that residue.
