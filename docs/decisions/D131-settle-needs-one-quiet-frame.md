## D131 — Settle trigger needs one quiet frame

**The settle machine's thresholds sat under the rest, so it needs a ratchet escape and a one-quiet-frame window.** The noise tracker admits only frames already under `tLo` to its still population, so it can lower the still level and never raise it. Seeded at 4.5 it converged to 4.5 to 7 while a resting card read `d` 5 to 8 under a bright lamp (brightness jitter, |Δluma| p90 of 6 to 8 at rest against 2 to 3 earlier). The settle rule then fired on 5 of 29 cards while every readout looked normal. Bright-lamp sessions keep 9 to 18% of frames under `tLo`, against 46 to 83% earlier. A window of four never fills either. The feeder rests a card for two to four frames, with calm frames around it. 13 of 28 cards never had four consecutive frames under `tHi`. A dimmer lamp was not the fix (about 87% at worse photograph quality). "No adaptation of a stillness detector answers a scene that is never still" was wrong. The scene was still, and the detector's idea of still was wrong in the one direction its ratchet could not move.

- **The ratchet's escape.** The noise window holds the frames with a card in view. When the frames the machine calls still are under `stillFractionMin` (0.30) of those frames, the still level comes from `restQuantile` (0.25) of all of them. `tHi` keeps its ratio. It applies only when that quantile is under the presence floor, since a change no smaller than a card arriving is not a rest. The fraction is over frames with a card in view. Otherwise an empty stand is still on every frame and would call the first cards a still majority. Measured both ways: zero verdicts change on the earlier sessions, and the bright-lamp sessions replay 31, 21, 45 and 85 fires against 5 live on two of them.
- **One quiet frame of the last three** (narrowing D84's two of four). A frame under `tLo` is still by definition and is photographed. A scene with no quiet frame at all still expires the stall clock, so D84's silent-loss guard is unchanged.
- **The cadence-trigger dual was deleted** (D144), so only these two rulings stand. The case it was built for, two or three sliding cards a run that stall, is the rescue's case (D144).
- **Built and mirrored.** The escape and window are in `app/src/motion.ts`, mirrored in `scripts/score-trace.py` and locked by `make docs-audit`'s `motion params` row, with an `escape` counter on the HUD (`docs/specs/motion-trigger.md` sections 2 and 7). At the rig, two boxes under the bright lamp gave about 97% where the shipped rule managed 17%. Every fire was under `d` 10. T9 pins the corpus. Not established: whether a `d` 5 to 8 frame reads clean is a fact about the JPEGs.
