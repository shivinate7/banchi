## D75 — Refuse a suspect crop

**`geometry.detect_card` answers "not found" honestly and cannot answer "found the wrong thing".** So the crop path asks the frame a second question. A crop under 30% of the frame that keeps under half its detail is not cut. The whole frame is sent. The same guard covers the crop-retry path, whose number bands are cut from that rectangle. A retry from a rules-text panel would enlarge rules text and call it the collector number.

- **Two legs, ANDed, because neither separates alone.** Over 867 real photographs, 858 correct crops span area 0.300 to 0.988 and detail 0.438 to 0.995. The 9 wrong ones span 0.068 to 0.270 and 0.152 to 0.435. The area gap is 0.270 to 0.300, and box 1's smallest correct crop sits exactly on 0.300. The detail gap is under a percent. A threshold in one gap is a number fitted to one rig. The AND refuses all 9 and none of the 858. Also, 27 correct crops sit under the detail line and are kept by the area leg. Detail is a ratio over the absolute-luminance gradient signal `geometry/detect.py` uses. So a dark backdrop cancels out.
- **The wrong crops are a card-shaped rectangle inside the card** (the rules-text panel), and the whole card is not among the candidates: the cards stand with a stack behind them. So this is a refusal and not a detector fix, since re-scoring cannot choose a rectangle that was never a candidate. A plain "under 10% of the frame" floor catches nothing extra and refuses a correct small card (T6's `_scene(scale=0.55)`).
- **Refusing is the cheap direction.** A whole frame costs image tokens and gets a correct answer. A false accept cuts the collector number out and gets a confident answer about nothing, which no confidence threshold catches (D23).
- **It lives in `identify/images.py:crop_refusal`, called from `prepare`,** the one place the bytes are made. `card_crop` and `crop_rect` stay pure. It returns a sentence, never a flag. `Prepared.crop_refused` carries it. The preflight prints the count apart from `no card found`, with the reasons, before any money. `#/runs` draws no rectangle and shows the sentence. The closing report lists `unfit crop` apart from `not detected`.
- **The aspect correction reaches both crop paths.** The border search comes back systematically short at the number end (registered aspect median 0.789 against a real card's 0.716). `geometry.corrected_bounds` holds the one computation, because `geometry/` may not import `identify/`. `identify/images.py:card_rect` delegates, and `registered_card` clamps to the canvas so no black bar shifts the bands. It is symmetric, so a box short only at the bottom recovers half its deficit. T6 has a real regression case (a box 10% short holds 0.088 of the number box before the fix and 0.157 after).

What is not established is a rate. Nine wrong crops in 867 frames from one rig is a count. Reopen for a rig whose correct crops are genuinely small: a nonzero `box unfit to crop to` where the crops look right asks for a re-measure.
