## D57 — Mark sold is one press, undoable

**Mark sold writes on one press and the row's control becomes `Undo`.** There is no confirm on a reversible act (`docs/DESIGN.md`). A sale is the most reversible write in the product: one route, two directions, no expiry, no listing hold. The modal's photograph was redundant, since the walk draws the selected copy's photograph (D38) and a copy's label walks to any other (D45). The `Undo` has no clock. It lasts until a newer sale replaces it or the sale is built on. The sale is built on once its order ships or closes, its photograph is reclaimed, or its box is buried (`docs/specs/undo.md` section 11).

- **Undo appears twice, and neither is redundant.** The row's slot is where the press was and where the eye is. The screen receipt survives unmount (stepping the walk, an empty query, a failed re-read all unmount the copy rows) and is the only place a refusal can be explained. Accessible names differ (`Undo <place>` and `Undo the sale at <place>`), so a screen reader can tell one sale's two ways back from two sales.
- **`already_sold` and `sold_origin_unknown` return `canUndo: false`,** so the row draws the plain word `sold`. A 32px slot has no room for the reason. The control renders inside the `sold` branch, because the optimistic `soldKeys` overlay makes a branch above it unreachable.
- **No `Retire` sits beside it,** because the server refuses to retire a sold card. Retirement keeps its panel, photograph and receipt-only undo, because it needs a reason (`retire_reason_invalid`), so its four buttons are the write's only input.
- **The overshoot guard is `busyKey` and nothing else.** Every control in the slot is disabled during a write, `doSell` returns early, and the slot shrinks to one right-packed control. The busy gate alone does not span a human double-tap against a local server. `#/fulfillment` displaces its controls instead, because `Pull` and `Mark sold` share a position (`app/tests/fulfillment.spec.ts` measures it). If a sale is recorded against the wrong copy, reach for that displacement.
- **It is a client change only.** `POST /inventory/<box>/<index>/sold` takes `{}` and `{"undo": true}` and answers `restores_to`. The guards belong to the write and not to the Fulfillment view.
- **The guard is six cases in `app/tests/inventory.spec.ts`** covering the receipt, the undo window, `canUndo` and `already_sold`. The spec had only asserted the buttons were visible.
