## D225 — Refunds subtract from Sales

**A reversed sale is not revenue, and a shortfall against today's market is not a loss.** Both are about what `#/revenue` admits it does not know. The plan is `docs/specs/sales-plan.md` sections 1 and 2.

- **Refunds subtract what the operator already told the store.** `closed_reason` rides on every order line's `progress` (D113). `salesOf` matches each line's SKU against the order's own `progress`, drops a line closed `not_shipping` (`store/orders.CLOSE_NOT_SHIPPING`, a refund or cancellation) from the total, and counts it in `refundExcluded`. It adds no route or wire change and does not widen `server/order_transport.py`'s allowlist. Measured on the real store, it catches nothing today. The store holds 796 `fulfilment` lines. Of these, 739 are `shipped_elsewhere`, 57 are `null`, and 0 are `not_shipping`. No "refund" string appears in 804 order payloads. So the screen states both counts and never conflates them. `revenue-verdict-canceled` counts orders the marketplace's own `Canceled` status excluded (D214), and `revenue-verdict-refunded` counts lines the operator's `not_shipping` closed. Both render even at zero, since a silent mechanism looks the same as one not wired up. The sentence says it is the operator's own note and a habit. It is not a guarantee. So it can undercount a real refund and never overcount one.
- **Against now, a market observation.** `Compare to today's market` calls `GET /pipeline/price-now?sku=...`. The first cut read `readings` (D189) alone, which covered 93 of 539 sold names and 0.9% of gross, with no sealed product (about 93% of the money). The source is the price-history archive (D219) tried first, reading each SKU's newest `month`-range bucket that has a `market` (skipping empty ones), with `readings` as the fallback. `source` (`archive` or `live`) travels with the answer, and the two ages mean different things (an archived bucket's day, a reading's fetch time).
- **Coverage is stated on screen.** `revenue-market-note` reads "N of M names have a price today, K do not". A row with no price says `no reading`, never a zero (D277).
- **Five rules, checked by `app/tests/revenue.spec.ts`.** Never a profit or a loss: no label says gain, loss, performance, beat or missed, only "above/below/even with what it sold for". Blind to cost, stated once beside the figure ("Blind to what any of this cost you."), since a card bought at two dollars and sold at eighteen would draw as a shortfall. A sign and a word, never a color (D278): `marketCompareOf` returns `above`, `below` or `even with`, and the cell prepends `+` or `−`. Today's price carries its own age (`ReadingAge`) whichever table answered. No price draws no figure.
- **The comparison is against the last sale under a name,** not an average (`Product.lastSku`, `Product.lastPrice`), since two printings can share a display name.
- **Measure before writing.** The first draft claimed refunds were reachable and that `readings` was the right source, and neither had been measured on the real store. Both were wrong. `not_shipping` was always the right field, and the archive-first order is a source swap and not a rewrite of the comparison.
