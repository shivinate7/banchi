## D68 — A departed card's label names its record

**`join.departed_label` ends on the store key, spelled `B3 #96`. Every renderer draws that key in the muted register.** Two copies can share a box and a neighbor text, so the label must name the record. Measured on the owner's store: two sold copies of `Vi, Peacekeeper` sat in box 1, at stored indices **67** and **106**. Both drew `Box 1 · departed`, with nothing beside them to tell them apart.

**D58's label is right, and it is not what was wrong.** A departed card is in no slot. Printing the slot number would print the number that now belongs to its successor. That is a lie about a shelf, so the label drops it. **What was wrong is that the disambiguating value was already in the payload, and every renderer threw it away.** `GET /search` returned `place.index` of 67 and 106 on those rows. D58 draws the distinction this needs. The stored index never moves, and it is the `/inventory/<box>/<index>` path. `Place.slot` is the countable number that shifts. The index is not a slot, so printing it is not the lie D58 refuses.

**It scales with sales.** On the owner's store, **11 of 12 departed records** sit in a `(SKU, box)` group. Four copies of Moonfall are in box 3, and three of Lightning Rush. The neighbor text underneath was the only thing separating them. That text is the shelf's fact, not the card's. It goes blank on copies whose neighbors have also departed.

The separator and spelling come from `place_text`, in the same module. `Position.label` is the only composer. The string travels verbatim on `aria-label`. No client code builds it.

**It ends on a key and never on a bare number.** `PositionLabel.tsx` promotes the last `·`-part of a label to the slot figure when it is all digits. `Box 3 · departed · 67` would draw **67 at 44px** in the slot column of a card that is in no slot. That is D58's lie, reintroduced by a renderer. `B3 #96` fails the guard, because it is not all digits.

**Why the `B`.** The key was first spelled `3/96`. The owner read that as a printed card number. The copies list shows the card's `NUMBER 114/166` a row apart. `<box>/<index>` and `<number>/<printedTotal>` are the same four characters in the same order. The box half stays. The copies list crosses boxes, so a bare `#96` is an address missing its first half. `B` is the one sigil that no count on these screens carries. `Card 17` counts cards. `Section 1 · #1–#108` counts cards too. `#41` in a neighbor row is an index in a sentence that says so. `B3 #96` cannot be read as either.

**Three composers carry the spelling, and they are the only ones.** `join.departed_label`, the departed branch of `join.where_phrase`, and `BoxBrowse.departedKey`. The walk's short cell composes the server's spelling, not the row's `{box}/{index}` map key. `PositionLabel.STORE_KEY` reads both shapes. A respelling that misses it does not fail. It silently draws the key as a position part. That is why that regex carries its own note.

**Unchanged on purpose:** the pooled label (`Pokémon code cards · pooled · 5/12`) and the `no label · 3/31` fault row. `BoxBrowse.shelvesOf` gives pooled records their own shelf, so the two forms never share a column. Every `{box}/{index}` that is a map key also stays. Those are the inventory map, the photo route and `<index>.jpg`. Machines resolve them. None sits beside a card number.

### What the screen needed

- **The walk's left cell clipped the key.** The full label measured 177px in a 169px cell at the shipped 11px Martian Mono. `BoxBrowse`'s `rowSlot` now composes `departed · B3 #96` from the row's own key. It joins the fallbacks `pooled · 5/2` and `no label · 3/31`. The box is what it spends. The walk is scoped to one shelf, named in the panel and in every section header. The short form measures 130.9px in a cell capped at `22ch` (169.4px). That leaves 38.5px spare, so nothing ellipsises. The old `departed · 3/96` measured 115.5px. The two extra characters cost 15.4px.
- **The copies list drew the whole string at payload size.** A departed row measured 154.2px against 133.6px for a live row. `PositionLabel` now peels the key off and renders it in the muted register, using `.position-storekey` on `.position-key`'s own `clamp` expression. No site declares a second scale. The row measures 105.7px.
- **The empty track.** `Place.slot` is null for two facts: a card that has left, and a box the server could not count. `app/src/server.ts:isDeparted` tells them apart. It checks `located`, a null `slot`, and a label, since the fault answers `label: null`. `PositionBar:sentenceOf` had answered both with the fault's words, `where this sits in the box is not known yet`. The sentence now reads `no longer in the box`.
- **The lens returns on a departed row, with its mark removed.** D118 governs the motion. The lens had been removed from departed rows on the press that sold the card. The panel then collapsed 98px and 131 elements moved, which the owner reported as screen shake. The lens is 85px at 1440x900. The rule that removed it was right about the mark and over-applied to the lens. The mark cannot draw a card that is in no place. The empty-track bullet above holds its own words: *"a true sentence under an empty track that still reads as a measurement that failed"*. That sentence is the OTHER bullet. The track is not empty now, because the section is drawn. The lens draws the box, and the box stays. The section the copy was in remains a run of slots. It is named from `section`, `section_start` and `section_end`, which survive when `slot` and `card` go null. The second scale reads `Section 1 · 10 slots · this copy is not in one`. The mark eases out and drops rather than being deleted between two frames. The owner chose an animation over a sentence. A deleted node cannot animate. The node that stays also holds the row's height steady.

**What is deliberately left.** D58 and D41 stand. No slot number is drawn for a card that has left. The void still holds the figure's column open. The label ends on `B3 #96` in the muted register. `join.departed_label` remains the one composer. The copies list draws the state word twice on every departed row. Once it is `.card-locations-state`, and once it is `Inventory.tsx:Action`'s fallback. Choosing which to delete is a D57 question, so it is recorded as a debt, not repaired here. The lone-copy call site has no state span.

**Tests.** Three assertions across `inventory.spec.ts` and `gallery.spec.ts` said a departed row has no bar. They now say the opposite, and they are amended in place with this argument beside them. A departed row is `is-gone`, and its lens carries `data-gone`. `.card-locations-row.is-gone.is-nobar` no longer marks a departed row.

**What would reopen it:** an operator who reads the muted section as a claim that the copy is still in it. The two states are a marked track against an unmarked grey one. They are legible beside each other in the copies list, and they were checked in both themes. A store where every copy of a card has departed draws no marked track anywhere. That is the case to look at first.

**D118 carries the measurements, the other three movers and the four guards.** Nothing about the stability floor is restated here.
