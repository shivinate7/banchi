## D154 — Lock camera settings that step

**The camera's automatic functions are inputs to the trigger's arithmetic, and the ones that step are locked.** Every threshold is a multiple of something the session measures (D81). Presence is the distance from the watch region at arming. `tLo` and `tHi` ride the session's median still-frame difference. A setting that moves between rigs is absorbed. A setting that moves while a session runs moves the baseline the thresholds derived from. Nothing reports it, and no constant in `motion.ts` can defend against that. So a camera setting is a repo fact with a measured reason per row and a reader. The rig is a Sony RX100 VII over micro-HDMI into a Cam Link 4K. The settings, derivations and checklist are in `docs/specs/motion-trigger.md` section 4. `scripts/score-trace.py camera` re-derives every figure. Figures were measured before `harness/traces/` was cut to ten. They are history. Where a rule rests on one, it is marked unmeasured.

- **Exposure matters most.** One 1/3 EV step moves the watch region by 8.5 to 22.5 luma levels, over `tLo` and over `presenceMin`. That holds on most traces that carry a baseline (unmeasured on the ten kept). So an AE step on an empty stand reads as a card arriving.
- **Gain is a cliff.** The still-frame floor is sensor and codec noise (lag-1 spatial correlation about 0.009), so gain reaches it directly. A slow drift is absorbed. A +1/3 stop step cost 48% of the fires after it. A +1.5 stop step never fires again, because D131's ratchet freezes `tLo`. Do not change D131 for this. Its escape refuses when its quantile sits at or above the presence floor. That is what makes it safe. `ISO AUTO` is what is banned.
- **The lamp is nearly no lever.** `log(typ) = -0.125 × log(level)` (r = -0.868), so halving the light raises the floor 9%. A lower ISO buys a quieter trigger at a factor of √2 per stop. Use the lamp for exposure headroom and spend the headroom on ISO.
- **Shutter and sharpening are null results.** At fire the card moves at about 184 sensor px/s, so 1/60 smears it 3.1 px of 3840 (1.3 px at the pipeline's resample). Open the shutter and spend it on ISO. A 12-pixel blur costs `d` 0.000, and the Picture Profile is locked only so it does not change mid-run.
- **Frame rate is the one free gain.** The stream is 24p. `4K Output Select` to `HDMI Only(30p)` is one menu item. Decimating to 12 fps costs 8.7% of fires and takes stalls from 12 to 76 (unmeasured on the ten kept; the 30p lock rests on it). Going up is not measured.
- **Autofocus and stabilization are locked.** The RX100 VII offers only Continuous AF and Manual Focus in movie mode, so manual is the only lock. A hunt costs `d` 3.03 at 1% focus breathing and 5.83 at 2%. `SteadyShot Active` is a 1.19x crop plus a digital warp.
- **The worst approach could not be measured on this rig.** The hand-to-card ramp is four to six frames (about 200 ms), under the trace's 1 Hz keyframe grid, and the per-frame bound of 12 to 23 straddles `presenceMin`. A 200 ms ramp cannot settle, so the settle rule does the work here and not presence. Check by eye at the rig whenever the feed changes (checklist step 6). `presenceMin` does not move.

No parameter in `motion.ts` moves: a setting is a rig fact and a threshold is a repo fact. The traces cannot prove the body was in manual, since drift is measured on still stretches and auto exposure steps only when the scene changes. Checklist step 2 settles which mode it was. Nothing here is validated at the rig.
