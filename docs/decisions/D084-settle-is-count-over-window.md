## D84 — A settle is a count over a window

**A settle is `stillFrames` of the last `stillWindow`, never a consecutive run, and it may only complete on a frame that is itself quiet.** A consecutive run is defeated absolutely by a two-frame alternation. That alternation is measured. A card motionless for 500 ms read `2.71 5.40 2.63 5.54 2.83 5.14 3.03 4.80 3.64 4.91`. Against a `tLo` of 4.18, that gave five runs of one and never two. The fire photographs the settle frame, so a capture must not come from a frame the machine had just called not-confidently-still. The cost is two frames of latency (about 84 ms at 24 fps). It is set against a 623 ms feeder period with about 400 ms of stillness in it. D131 narrowed the count to one quiet frame of the last three, since 2-of-3 and 3-of-5 recovered fewer cards here. Every defect sat in the first two seconds after arming. The four losses were established by looking. JPEGs downsampled to the trace's 38x28 grid matched the recorded frames at MAD 13 to 19. The orphan scenes matched at 23.7 to 30.2.

- **The stall clock is cleared by a completed settle.** It was cleared by any single frame under `tLo` while a fire needed two in a row. The alternation reset it every other frame, so `stalled` never fired across four unphotographed cards. `stalled` is the one signal meaning "something is there and I am not capturing it". It now reports one stall per session on a real card, with zero false positives over 67 good captures and 217 earlier verdicts. It is deliberately not a capture. Two of the four losses were genuinely moving, with the operator's hand on the card. No threshold should photograph them. Silent losses become photographs or loud sentences (section 5.5).
- **`presenceMin` is 16.0, sized to a hand.** D81's 8.0 was set by illumination drift. The drift does not survive re-measurement, since an undisturbed stand does not creep (per-second worst `dBase` of 2.1 to 2.8). Everything above 3 is the operator's hand entering with the first card. It holds still two frames on the way in, so 8.0 fired on the bare stand. 16.0 is 1.4 times over the worst approach measured (11.15). It is 2 times under the quietest card (32.5). It removes exactly the two junk fires and nothing else.
- **That constant is a debt and not a design.** `presenceK × dTypical` sits at 6 to 10 on this rig. So this floor decides every verdict, and D81's adaptive half decides none. It stays constant because it must clear a hand, whose size in frame no session statistic measures. Three quantities re-derive it on a new rig: the idle stand, the worst approach and the quietest card. Nothing in the machine measures the second.
- **Enforced.** `make docs-audit`'s `motion params` row reconciles `app/src/motion.ts`'s `DEFAULT_PARAMS` against `scripts/score-trace.py`'s mirrored constants in both directions. It has an accounted-for list for the one parameter the scorer does not carry. A comment naming a check that does not exist is worse than none. T9 grades that the floor refuses exactly the settles that photographed the bare stand and nothing else that fired. It asserts by time, not count, because a count passes if the floor climbs high enough to refuse two cards instead.

Reopen for a rig whose hand approach reads above 16 or a card that reads below it. The answer measures the approach the machine can take for itself, not a fourth constant.
