## D288 — Repeated actions are labeled icons

**A common, repeated action is an icon button with a required label.** A press that spends money or cannot be undone keeps its words. The split is the cost of a misread and not frequency: Send is common too and keeps its word. The icon list is Mark sold, Undo, Retire, Edit, Delete, Copy, Download, Open, Close, Filter, Sort and any action of that kind. Words stay on Send, Identify, Stand down and any press of that kind. `docs/specs/iconography.md` carries the full rule (section 2 is the seven-step order, with the Fulfiller exemption, phone edge cases and dense-row limits), the per-lane map of every press, the icon set and its collisions.

- **`IconButton` (`app/src/kit/index.tsx`) requires `label`,** since an icon with no word is a guess and TypeScript refuses a call site that omits it. `label` is the tooltip text and the accessible name, unless a longer `name` overrides it for a dense row. `name` must contain `label` (WCAG 2.5.3), and a dev-mode `console.error` names a call site that breaks this. Other props are `tone="danger"`, `pressed`, `busy`, `badge` (absolutely positioned and `aria-hidden`, so the caller's `name` carries the count) and `kbd` (sets `aria-keyshortcuts`). `href` with `target` and `rel` renders an `<a>` with the same face, hit area, tooltip and name, because a link opened through `window.open` loses middle-click and the browser's own "Open link in new tab".
- **The visual face is 28px and the 40px thumb floor (D117) is a `::before`,** `max(40px, 100%)`, so it expands the clickable region without existing in layout. An outer 40px box broke one control height in a field row (`SearchField` grew on the first keystroke, D118). It reuses `.bn-btn`, so it inherits D50's cursor, response and press floors.
- **Neither the pseudo nor `.bn-fchip-clear` centers itself with `transform`.** A `translate(-50%, -50%)` promotes the element to its own compositor layer, and a real Chromium click past its box inside that layer does not always reach it, though `elementFromPoint` reads it fine. Both center by inset alone (`min(0px, calc((100% - 40px) / 2))` on all four sides, and `top: 0; bottom: 0; margin: auto 0`).
- **The tooltip is a DOM child of the button and never a portal** (`app/tests/icon-button.spec.ts` finds it with `button.querySelector('.bn-icon-tip')`). It is `position: fixed`, computed by `reposition()` on pointer-enter, focus and the long-press timer, clamped inside the viewport and flipped below when there is no room, so a sheet's `overflow: hidden` does not clip it. Visibility is CSS: `:hover` sits under `@media (hover: hover)`, so a tap leaves no phantom hover, and `:focus-visible` and `[data-tip-open]` cover keyboard and long-press. A long-press that opens the tooltip calls `preventDefault()` on its own `touchend` so it never also presses the button.
- **The Fulfiller gets words for the overlay Close.** `PageRoute` (`app/src/kit/Page.tsx`) carries `persona`, and `OverlayFrame` reads it from context (a portal keeps React context). His Close is a plain worded `Button` at a literal 20px (`.bn-overlay-close-worded`), the `docs/DESIGN.md` floor and not `--ff-fs-base`. His route renders outside the shell through its own branch in `App.tsx`, which must wrap its view in `PageRouteContext.Provider`, or every overlay opened there draws the icon-only Close.
- **Icons live in `app/src/kit/Icon.tsx`** in the 24-unit, 1.75-stroke idiom: `sold`, `pencil`, `eraser`, `eyeOff`, `sortAsc`, `sortDesc`, `moveTo`, `grip`. `ICON_MEANINGS` has a row for every icon the vocabulary draws, and `kit-adoption` fails a commit that drops a row or names a vocabulary icon `PATHS` does not draw (`iconMeaningsGap`). Review at magnification found glyphs that read wrong: `grip`'s dots were under 1.2px at 16px, sort bars all drew one width (they now taper), and `moveTo` looked like the "sign in" bracket. Every state renders on `#/gallery` ("Icon button", between "Buttons" and "Keycaps"). The gallery's toast specimen must render the real `IconButton`, or `axe` never catches a toast contrast regression.
- **Seven kit-owned controls are converted:** the overlay Close, the Toaster dismiss, the FacetChip clear, the SearchField clear, `ReloadButton`, the compact `FilterBar` trigger and `SortControl`'s direction toggle (the sort key stays in words). Their old CSS (a bordered field look, a second count pill, a second hover paint) was deleted and not patched around. That exposed the Toaster dismiss at 1.83:1 in light theme, because `.bn-icon-face`'s fixed color beat `color: inherit`, and a `FilterBar` badge that no longer reached a screen reader, since the badge is `aria-hidden`. `FilterBar` now passes a `name` stating the label and the count.
- **`kit-adoption`'s `R2-icon-only-button` rule (`scripts/kit-adoption.mjs`) refuses three shapes** outside `app/src/kit/`, the Fulfiller's files and Gallery. *(a)* `iconOnly` on `Button`, read by presence and including a spread. *(b)* A `<button>` or `<a role="button">` whose whole visible content is an icon (`<Icon>`, `<svg>`, a bare `×`, `✕` or `✖`), through a wrapper, an expression or a ternary of two icons, or with no visible content and only an `aria-label`. A real word anywhere in the content clears it, even one the reader cannot read. *(c)* A `Button` whose literal label starts with a vocabulary verb (the list includes Hold, Release, Reveal, Hide and Clear, matched case-insensitively and read through `{'Undo'}` and `Undo {n}`) when its `variant` cannot be shown to always be `primary` or `danger-solid`. The variant reader treats one unresolved branch of `??`, `||`, `&&` or a ternary as making the whole unresolved, so unknown means not provably worded. A dynamic variant must split into two JSX branches. The rule's offenders are a shrinking list in `scripts/kit-adoption-allow.json` under lane `icons`, keyed file to rule (D275), so a new icon-only control in an already-listed file passes silently until that file's entry is removed. `make kit-adoption-selftest` proves both directions, including permanent evasion fixtures.
- **`app/tests/icon-button.spec.ts` (`make design-check`)** checks that the 40px hit area reaches past the face, the tooltip shows on hover and focus and hides after a click, showing it moves nothing (D118), the overlay Close tooltip is not clipped by a sheet at 1440 or 390, a long-press does not also press, the toast dismiss glyph clears 4.5:1 in both themes, and the anchor form carries `href`, `target` and `rel`, follows on Enter, and matches the button form.

Converting a screen's existing controls is each screen lane's work. The words-or-icon call for every press the map found is settled here and is not made again per screen.
