## D184 — Re-baseline only on proof of a gain step

**The presence gate reads a distance from the arm-time baseline, and an auto-exposure step is a distance too, so nothing could tell the two apart.** A gain step is the baseline times one number, and a card is not. The machine re-baselines only on that proof.

- **The test.** A frame that clears the presence floor is asked whether it is the baseline times one number. `k` is the median per-cell ratio to the baseline over the cells both frames hold between a toe and a shoulder. Scale the baseline up by `k` and read the mean absolute residual, in the frame's own units. A gain step leaves the plate's noise and a card leaves its pattern. The bound is `presenceK` times the session's own still-frame difference, the multiple the presence floor already rides, so no threshold moved. Over 353 fired frames that clear the floor across ten sessions, the smallest residual on every session is 4.1 times the bound or more (5.3 on the 85/85 run). The one frame under it is the plate's own dark disc displaced, and T9 pins it by time. Frame units were measured against baseline units: dividing the frame down put sixteen real cards at 11.0 to 12.0 against a bound of 8.04.
- **The ruling.** A uniform rescale of the baseline over the presence floor is `suppressed:uniform`, and the frame becomes the baseline. This is the one place the machine re-baselines on its own. `MotionControls.rebaseline`'s rule stands, since this is a proof that nothing on the stand changed. None of the 353 fired frames passes it. Adopting the frame keeps a hand under the floor after a step. The novelty gate asks the same question of the last fired frame, so a step over a photographed card is `suppressed:unchanged`. `d` is untouched. A slow ramp under `tLo` per frame is not covered (`docs/specs/motion-trigger.md` section 5). Three parameters shape the comparable set and none is a threshold: `uniformMinShare` 0.25, `uniformToe` 8 and `uniformShoulder` 247. They are mirrored into `scripts/score-trace.py` and reconciled by `make docs-audit`'s `motion params` row, which now mirrors `tNovel` too. The manual-exposure lock stays: this makes the trigger survive its absence, and the lock keeps the presence floor meaning one thing for a box.
- **Refused, each measured.** Normalizing against a reference region outside the watch region: traces store only the watch region, and the surround measured from 2,535 photographs drifts within a sitting (a uniformity guard would decline almost every verdict, and without one the factor is wrong by up to 2.5 times). Normalizing the watch region by its own median ratio always: a bright card on a dark plate is scaled to plate amplitude, and 77 of 661 fired cards fall under the floor. `geometry.detect_card` as a gate is exposure-invariant but costs 18 to 42 ms and runs in Python on the server while the trigger runs in the browser. The process boundary is the objection, and it stays the per-capture crop.
- **Not established.** No real exposure step has been recorded on this rig. The step here is a multiplicative model with the plate's noise. The first trace saved with the body in an auto mode, `scripts/score-trace.py gain` over it, and the HUD's `uniform` count on a box validate it. It governs `app/src/motion.ts`, `scripts/score-trace.py`, `harness/tests/t9_traces.py` and `app/tests/motion.spec.ts`. It does not reopen D19, D131 or D144, and D81's presence is a distance that has a shape.
