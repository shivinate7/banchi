## D186 — Per-card cost divides by cards submitted

**Divide by the cards actually submitted, never the cards in the run.** A cache hit is billed nothing, so a run that served a third of itself from cache reports a per-card price a third too low. The error makes the dearer setting look cheap: cache hits accumulate on a re-joined box, and re-joins concentrate on the incumbent reading. `identify`'s preflight already prints `cache hits` and `to send` separately and estimates on the second. Measured over every run with a usage block, on submitted cards only: 2,179 input tokens a card at max-edge 900 and 2,641 at 1200 (a delta of 462, not 240, so the store-wide saving is $0.60 and not $0.36). The inflation reaches 4.26 times on one run.

Compare a parameter on a metric that parameter can move. The queued count is `no_catalog_row` in every case cited, and that is catalog completeness, not reading difficulty. No downscale can make a catalog row exist. The resolution-sensitive code is `number_unread_name_matched` (D35), 212 of 2,535 store-wide (8.36%) against 0.47% for the queued count.

- **A design needs the power to see the effect.** The comparison is paired (the same cards at both readings), so only discordant pairs carry information, and six all one way is the floor for p under 0.05. Against a 0.47% base rate, 100 cards gives 38.7% odds both arms return zero and 0.0% power to see a 1.5 times change. A paired 100 on `number_unread_name_matched` detects a change of 1.7 times or more (36% power against a doubling, 67% at n=200).
- **A cross-run comparison of two readings is confounded twice.** Both 900 runs are all Riftbound, and `number_unread_name_matched` ranges 3.4% to 11.7% between boxes at a fixed reading. Pokemon composes a denominator into its number key and Riftbound does not.
- **D32 stands and its frontier table is unaffected,** since it prices configs per box from 20 real frames. The gates corpus's `GateB-note1` record (19 of 40 cards disagreeing on `finish` between 900 and 1200) is the standing proof that the reading changes the answer. `finish` is no longer sent to the model.
