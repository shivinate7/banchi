## D23 — Rarity claim catches confident misreads

**The capture screen's multi-select rarity claim, scoped by game, does four jobs.**

1. **Cross-check: `rarity_claim_mismatch`.** It is emitted in the variant ladder, not in routing. Routing asks whether a card is trusted enough to list, and the ladder asks which row it is. It filters candidate rows to the claimed rarities and reviews when none survive. It catches what no confidence threshold fires on: right name, wrong digits (`051/197` for `031/197`). Rung 0 never consults it, because a human who looked at the photograph outranks a claim about the stack.
2. **Narrow the finish chips.** Offered chips are the union of `finish_by_rarity` over the claimed rarities. An empty claim narrows nothing. It never auto-selects, not even with one finish left, because a manufactured claim makes ladder rung 2 (`CATALOG_FORCED`) unreachable. Excluded chips are drawn and unselectable, so the operator sees what the claim cost. `finish_by_rarity` stays a superset of what any one export proves. If it is narrowed to one export's observations, a legitimate stack becomes unclaimable.
3. **Prompt injection is measured and switched off.** With best-case claims it cost $0.17 and lost on every watched axis: holdout down 1.5 points, high-confidence misses up two. `BANCHI_T1_RARITY=1` re-enables it. The condition is a rig-photo measurement. The `SYSTEM_PROMPT` rule not to infer finish from rarity is what a careless clause breaks.
4. **Rank.** `pipeline/join.py:rank_by_claims` orders candidate rows best first by how many capture claims each agrees with (rarity, `metadata_finish`, set hint). It is presentation only: it adds no row, picks none, and leaves a card with no favored row tied. `join_batch` widens `found.rows` by name when the number's own rows never satisfy a present rarity claim. `do_review_catalog`'s free-text search calls the same function, so a claim scores once. Rung 0 is exempt by construction, since a human-answered card resolves to one row.

A typed rarity is evidence, not fact: two agreeing signals release it, and disagreement is a review reason.
