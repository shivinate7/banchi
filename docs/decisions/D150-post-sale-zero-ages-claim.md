## D150 — A post-sale zero reading ages the claim

**A reading taken after a sale confirms the sale.** `pushed` has no drawdown. `cmd_emit` writes it, `cmd_reconcile` moves it to `staged`, and `cmd_join` draws `staged` down by the rise in live. So after an import lands, the claim counts copies TCGplayer already sold. The copies on hand are committed against a shelf that no longer exists. A sale is the only event that proves a sent copy left. `cli/resolve.py:_copies_out` ages the claim by the SKU's sales that the export confirms:

    out[sku] = max(live, claim - sold)

- **The reading answers two questions.** A reading with copies live confirms every sale of the SKU, at any time. A reading of nothing confirms only the sales made before it (`Inventory.sales_before`). A zero reading four days after the copies sold is the sale's own result. D115's rule pointed the other way. A sale after a zero reading is not aged, because the reading cannot show that the copy was one the claim counts. The old gate treated "never live" and "live, sold, now reads zero" as one case.
- **Neither arm double-counts.** The two are a `max` over independent estimates, never a sum. `claim` is never decremented by a sale. Landed live with sales after it (4 @ T, 2 sold later): 2. Landed live, reading after the sales (2 @ T+): 2. Sold out, reading after the sale (0 @ T+, 1 sold): 0. Pushed, never read, 1 sold: 1. A date rule that ages only by sales before the reading answers 4 for the first case and strands two copies. So this is a second arm on the gate, not a replacement.
- **Three rules, one place each.** `Listing.live_reading` picks the reading to believe. `Listing.sales_pending` says which counted sales survive it. `Listing.reading_taken_at` dates it. Its branches mirror `live_reading` exactly, stampless included. So `_copies_out` never asks whether a sale came before a reading that is not the one used.
- **D54's re-emit idempotence stays.** Four pushed, one sold, export silent: no reading exists (`live_as_of is None`), `sales_before` counts nothing, and no fifth row is offered. Dropping the gate outright turns that case red (expected 4, actual 5). Three mutation arms were run: the old gate restored, no gate, and the date comparison reversed.
- **Nothing writes, and reconcile is not the answer.** The operator does not run `reconcile --live`. The aging is derived per resolve from two stamps the store already holds. The false sentence "every copy in this run is already listed" is removed, because the rows are offered.
- **Scope.** A zero reading means not live. A copy stuck in TCGplayer's Staged list is out of scope by the owner's choice. The store assumes that a copy is either live or not. Staged is not modeled.
