## D123 — Design to the column, not to zoom

**Above the desk a screen asks its column width.** Browser zoom is not a density lever: it is a similarity transform (80% zoom is every length times 0.8 and every breakpoint times 1.25), so density is already solved per device and reversibly. A `banchi.density` token would only add selectivity (shrink the chrome, keep the photograph, spare the Fulfiller), and it is deferred until the owner wants that. Zoom widens the effective viewport, so it buys more stretch and not less. `#/pricing` at 1920 had the card identity 924px wide and the price field, the hero of the row (D86), 68px.

- **Below 1024, `@media`. Above 1024, a cap or a container.** Every screen but the Fulfiller's draws inside `.bn-shell-main`, the viewport minus 236px, or minus 64px railed. Those differ by 172px, wider than the gap between two ladder steps, so a `min-width: 1024px` cannot tell a 788px column from a 960px one. A cap is measured against the column the shell gave, and holds beside the sidebar, beside the rail and at any zoom. `--bn-page-w` (1600) is the ceiling and `--bn-page-w-rows` (1344) is for a list of rows. A screen lowers its own with `--bn-page-max`, which is not a token, since it is a screen's word. Pricing sets 1120, sized to the measured ink.
- **`:not([data-rail='true'])` is not the mechanism.** The attribute lies between 768 and 1023, where `App.css` rails the shell by media query, and it costs every declaration twice and hardcodes the 172px delta. `ReviewQueue.css` keeps its pattern only because `container-type` would capture its `position: fixed` sheet.
- **The vocabulary is enforceable.** A `min-width` is the step and a `max-width` is the step minus one. `@media` and `@container` are separate namespaces, since a 640px pane and a 640px window are different quantities. `docs/DESIGN.md` carries the register. `make docs-audit`'s `breakpoints` row is mechanical (an integer against an integer). `breakpoint columns` is advisory, because whether a `min-width` asks the wrong question depends on what the rule does.
- **Not a defect:** `app/src/Fulfillment.css` reads `--bn-control-h*` zero times, and no control there is under 44px (the shortest is 60px). Coupling his contractual floors to a token would check nothing.
- **The caps have a re-checker.** `wide.spec.ts` runs in `make design-check`, and `.github/workflows/check.yml` runs that suite as a second job, with D50's floors, D117's thumb floor, D118's stability floor and the Fulfiller's constraints.

Reopen for a screen that needs more than 1536px of content, or an operator who wants the density lever.
