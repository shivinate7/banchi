## D248 — Turn end never runs the harness

**There is no Stop hook: nothing runs at turn end.** The harness runs on the commit path (`make check`, where D161 puts it first) and in CI only. It cost about 25 seconds on every turn, against nothing now, on the machine whose capture server and rig also run.

- **A cheap check picked at random costs more than it buys.** No single fast probe stands in for ten tests over `store`, `pipeline`, `identify`, `server`, `codes` and their joins. `cd app && npx tsc --noEmit` only catches TypeScript errors, and most turns never touch `app/`. A turn-end check unrelated to what changed teaches the reader to ignore it (a guard must see its subject, and a guard that fires on nothing wrong is spent).
- **The cost is accepted and stated.** A session that never commits can carry a broken pipeline through many turns until the next commit or CI run. Nothing else inside a turn notices.
- **The no-op Stop hook script is deleted.** It only exited 0, and `make status` has no "turn gate" line.
- **Code cards fold into one rules file.** The owner ruled the code-card track is a feature they have not used. It is not a second track. The owner asked for one place to mark it dormant. `code-card-fork/CLAUDE.md` is a relative symlink to `../CLAUDE.md` (D135's pattern), and `code-card-fork/AGENTS.md` resolves one hop further to the same file, so no document reads a copy. `CLAUDE.md`'s "Code cards (dormant feature)" section is the one place that marks dormancy, and it is not repeated elsewhere. Nothing is deleted from the build: `codes/`, `#/codes`, harness T8 and the QR decode keep running. D14's structural half is untouched (`docs/map.py`'s `TRACKS`, `codes/` as its own package, `scripts/decision-context.py`'s track banner), and `TRACKS`'s `"rules"` field still resolves through the symlink. The audit rows `codex hooks` and `repo map` do not read that file's content.
