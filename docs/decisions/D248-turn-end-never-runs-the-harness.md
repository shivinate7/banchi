## D248 — Turn end never runs the harness

**The Stop hook stays cheap: `scripts/stop-gate.sh` runs nothing at turn end.** The harness runs on the commit path (`make check`, where D161 puts it first) and in CI only. It cost about 25 seconds on every turn, against 0.006 seconds now, on the machine whose capture server and rig also run.

- **A cheap check picked at random costs more than it buys.** No single fast probe stands in for ten tests over `store`, `pipeline`, `identify`, `server`, `codes` and their joins. `cd app && npx tsc --noEmit` only catches TypeScript errors, and most turns never touch `app/`. A turn-end check unrelated to what changed teaches the reader to ignore it (a guard must see its subject, and a guard that fires on nothing wrong is spent).
- **The cost is accepted and stated.** A session that never commits can carry a broken pipeline through many turns until the next commit or CI run. Nothing else inside a turn notices.
- **The hook stays on the roster, doing no work,** so `--status` still answers why nothing is running and `make status`'s "turn gate" line still reads it. `PKMNSCAN_GATE=off` stays recognized though it changes nothing, so a shell profile that sets it is not silently turned into a no-op with no trace.
- **Code cards fold into one rules file.** The owner ruled the code-card track is a feature they have not used, not a second track, and asked for one place to mark it dormant. `code-card-fork/CLAUDE.md` is a relative symlink to `../CLAUDE.md` (D135's pattern), and `code-card-fork/AGENTS.md` resolves one hop further to the same file, so no document reads a copy. `CLAUDE.md`'s "Code cards (dormant feature)" section is the one place that marks dormancy, and it is not repeated elsewhere. Nothing is deleted from the build: `codes/`, `#/codes`, harness T8 and the QR decode keep running. D14's structural half is untouched (`docs/map.py`'s `TRACKS`, `codes/` as its own package, `scripts/decision-context.py`'s track banner), and `TRACKS`'s `"rules"` field still resolves through the symlink. The audit rows `codex hooks` and `repo map` do not read that file's content.
