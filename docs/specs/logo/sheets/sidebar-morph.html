the sidebar — the lockup open, the brackets closed

The owner’s idea, drawn: the lockup when the sidebar is open, collapsing to the bare brackets when it is a rail. §1 already calls that second state the mark — “with the card removed the same brackets become an empty slot, which is the in-product mark.”

The lockup REPLACES the wordmark and the tagline. An earlier draft of this sheet drew it beside Banchi and every card has an address, which was wrong twice: the lockup already says the name, in two scripts, so the row read as twice as busy as it would be — and it faked the sizing, squeezing the lockup into what was left after the text instead of letting it own the row.

The size was an open question and §16 settled it: kanji 40. With 212px of usable width the lockup fits anywhere from the kanji-32 floor up to kanji 80; the three candidates are still drawn below because the comparison is what the answer rests on, and 40 is captioned as the one that ships. The rail is 64px and cannot hold the lockup at all: at kanji 22 the block is 57px and two of its three measures have already failed. That is why the rail gets the brackets rather than a smaller lockup — and, since 2026-09-06, why it gets them by morphing into them rather than by swapping to a second drawing.

The morph is the shipped one, drawn. Both ends come out of the same taperParts at the same 301 samples — the rail end is railArm(), the same call with tip = 1 — so the two d strings share a command structure and the filmstrip below is a straight point-wise lerp, the one Lockup.tsx runs. This sentence was here before it was true: the filmstrip used to interpolate the parameters and re-sample, which drew a tapered rail end the product does not contain.
