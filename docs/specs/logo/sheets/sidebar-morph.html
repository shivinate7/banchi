the sidebar — the lockup open, the brackets closed

The owner’s idea, drawn: the lockup when the sidebar is open, collapsing to the bare brackets when it is a rail. §1 already calls that second state the mark — “with the card removed the same brackets become an empty slot, which is the in-product mark.”

The lockup REPLACES the wordmark and the tagline. An earlier draft of this sheet drew it beside Banchi and every card has an address, which was wrong twice: the lockup already says the name, in two scripts, so the row read as twice as busy as it would be — and it faked the sizing, squeezing the lockup into what was left after the text instead of letting it own the row.

So the size is now an open question rather than a forced one. With 212px of usable width the lockup fits anywhere from the kanji-32 floor up to kanji 80, and three candidates are drawn below. The rail is 64px and cannot hold the lockup at all: at kanji 22 the block is 57px and two of its three measures have already failed. That is why the rail gets the brackets rather than a smaller lockup.

The morph is drawn, not described. Both brackets come from the same taperParts at the same point count, so the path interpolates directly — the filmstrip below is the real intermediate geometry at five points, not a cross-fade standing in for one.
