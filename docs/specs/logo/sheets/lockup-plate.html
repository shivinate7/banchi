the lockup as a plate — the icon with the name where the card goes

Nothing here is new geometry. The tile is the committed TILE path out of app/src/kit/markGeometry.ts, the brackets are the committed tapered construction, and the six palettes are read out of markPalettes.ts — which logo parity already reconciles against §9. What changes is the tile's aspect and what sits inside the brackets.

The tile is SPLICED, not stretched. Widening a quintic superellipse by scaling shears its corners into ellipses and it stops being the same shape; splicing a straight run in at the vertical centreline keeps every corner byte-identical to the icon's at the same drawn height.

番地 is live text in IBM Plex Sans JP, subset to two glyphs — 1,108 bytes. Not outlines: the macOS agreement governs extracting Hiragino's shapes into a file, and displaying a font you are licensed for is a different act. An open face at 1.1 KB removes the question rather than answering it.
