the lockup — 番地 over BANCHI

Section 3 has specified this since it was written and nothing has ever drawn it. The table there is four LOCKED values, two RANGED, and one instruction — "tracked until it is exactly as wide as 番地" — that has two different answers depending on what "as wide as" is measured on.

Everything here is positioned by INK, never by line box. Hiragino's font box is 88 up / 12 down on a 100 em; Manrope's is 92 up / 23 down — 115 units on the same em. Stacking the two by their line boxes puts the optical centre in the wrong place by design, so every measurement below is actualBoundingBox taken from this machine's own fonts at render time and printed under the specimen it produced.
